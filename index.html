Why the math demands it
At 8–9% organic growth, $9M compounds to roughly $18M in eight years, short of the target. Reaching $27M takes closer to 15% a year. Some of that gap is acquisitions. The rest has to come from capacity: more revenue per person, without proportional headcount and G&A underneath it.
You've already bought growth the expensive way once
The last capacity step-change (the building, heat and cooling, added leadership) pushed G&A up about 4 points. On today's revenue that's roughly $350K a year, permanently. Necessary then. But if the next 3x is bought the same way, the $3–6M EBITDA never materializes. The overhead eats it.
What the alternative is worth
Every point of NOI on $9M is $90K a year. Climbing back toward your 16% peak is worth $300–450K annually. And at a 5–7x multiple, margin that holds through the 3x is worth seven figures at exit.
