The 2027 picture

Creators, not consumers.

Grow the business without growing overhead by turning everyone into creators with AI, not consumers of it. That sentence is already yours. The rest of this paragraph, what March 2028 actually looks like at Great Lakes, gets written by the leadership team in the session. Some raw material: every department running one tool its own people built; the slow handoffs in the customer flow, wherever discovery finds them, moving in hours; tribal knowledge out of Slack and into standards a new hire can run on; AI numbers on the weekly scorecard. Revenue growing faster than headcount.

The three things

What most businesses aren't thinking about.

1

Foundation

Data lives in multiple places (LMN, HubSpot, spreadsheets). Context lives in Slack and in people's heads: pricing calls, standards, the decisions behind them. Everything built on top inherits whatever that layer is. Most companies skip it and buy tools.

2

Strategy

Five questions only the leadership team can answer, on one page it ratifies: Where are we, honestly? Where do we want to go? What is slowing us down? What will it take to get there? And what don't we know yet: the gaps in knowledge, and the possibilities nobody in the building has seen. Who owns it and which weekly number it reports to are answered here too. An outsider's job is to ask these well, not answer them.

3

Capability

The team's ability to build, not just use. There is a ladder: consumers of AI, creators with AI, agents on your data. The headcount math only changes on the second rung, and almost no company plans the climb. The skill of the team is the asset. The tools are rented.

The rock, in EOS form

Two things can come from outside: the form (one deliverable, one owner, a leading number, a done-definition, no "ands") and the possibilities nobody inside has seen yet. Everything else is content, and content has to be yours or it won't get done. So this is a draft v0, not a decision. A specific agent as the rock would be guessing: the proposal queue was observed, not something leadership has named. Strategy first; builds come off the roadmap.

The math

One line.

Every point of NOI on $9M is $90K a year. Growing without adding overhead is how you earn it, and you do that by becoming creators with AI, not consumers of it. The path back to the 16% peak is worth $300–450K a year; at a 5–7x exit multiple, margin that holds through the 3x is worth seven figures.
